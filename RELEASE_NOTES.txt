anonssh v0.2.0-beta.1

Pre-release highlights:
- In-process Arti Tor routing with isolated SSH circuits.
- Real --exit-country routing through Arti StreamPrefs.
- Keyboard-interactive PAM/OTP/2FA authentication.
- OpenSSH, PEM, PKCS#8, PuTTY, and encrypted private-key loading.
- Command stdin forwarding and reliable EOF handling.
- Reliable terminal restoration and temporary Arti state cleanup.
- Ctrl+C cancellation during Tor bootstrap and connection.
- Automatic lyrebird/pluggable-transport discovery and validation.

Security note: selecting an exit country reduces the anonymity set and can reduce connection reliability. Tor remains vulnerable to sufficiently capable traffic-correlation observers.
